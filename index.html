How it works

From job site to insurance-ready in seconds

Step 01
Describe the damage or upload a photo
Select the job type, enter basic damage details, or upload a site photo. AI identifies damage category and class automatically.
Step 02
AI generates a complete IICRC-standard checklist
RestoreDocAI cross-references IICRC S500, S520, and carrier documentation requirements to build a complete, section-by-section checklist.
Step 03
Download as PDF or share with your team
Export a carrier-ready PDF with your company branding, or share a live link with your crew via a 6-digit PIN — no accounts needed for field techs.
