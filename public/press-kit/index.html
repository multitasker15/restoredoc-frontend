RestoreDocAI is the leading AI-powered documentation platform for restoration contractors. The company's software enables field technicians to generate IICRC-compliant insurance checklists in under five minutes using photo analysis and AI — dramatically reducing claim denials and accelerating reimbursement timelines for water, fire, mold, storm, and biohazard restoration jobs. Backed by $8.2M in Series A funding, RestoreDocAI serves over 1,200 restoration companies across all 50 states. Headquartered in Denver, CO, the company was founded in 2023 by CEO Oliver Ford, a 12-year restoration industry veteran. Learn more at restoredocai.com.
