The Problem

Insurance claim denials cost the US restoration industry an estimated $4.2B annually. The leading cause is inadequate or non-compliant documentation — field technicians are expected to produce IICRC-standard reports under time pressure, often on mobile devices, without standardized tools.

The average restoration company spends 52 minutes per job on documentation and still sees 23% of claims denied or delayed on first submission.

The Solution

RestoreDocAI gives field technicians a mobile-first, PIN-protected tool to document any restoration job in minutes. The AI analyzes site photos, identifies damage materials, and generates a complete 8-section IICRC-compliant checklist — formatted exactly the way insurance adjusters require it.

No app download. No training required. Share a link, enter a PIN, start documenting.

Core Capabilities

  • AI photo analysis — identifies damage type, severity, and affected materials from job site photos
  • Auto-generated IICRC checklists covering all 8 required sections (S500, S520, S700 standards)
  • Xactimate-ready scope of work export for direct adjuster submission
  • PIN-protected team links — no app download required for field techs
  • Real-time dashboard with usage analytics and claim approval tracking
  • PDF export with full chain of custody documentation

Market Context

The US property restoration market is a $210B industry with over 45,000 active contractors. It is largely un-digitized at the field documentation layer — most teams still rely on handwritten notes, personal photos, and email chains to submit claims. RestoreDocAI addresses the last mile of the insurance claims process: the 72 hours after a job begins, when documentation quality determines whether a contractor gets paid in full, and on time.
