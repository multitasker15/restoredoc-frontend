Oliver Ford
CEO & Co-Founder
RestoreDocAI — Denver, CO
IICRC WRT Certified 12 Yrs Restoration Industry Series A CEO AI & Insurance Tech

Oliver Ford spent twelve years on the operations side of the restoration industry before founding RestoreDocAI in 2023. As Vice President of Operations at a multi-state water and fire restoration company, he oversaw more than 400 jobs per year and watched firsthand as documentation failures — not poor workmanship — became the leading cause of delayed and denied insurance claims.

"We were doing excellent work in the field and getting paid 70 cents on the dollar because a moisture reading was in the wrong column or a photo didn't show the right angle," Ford said. "The IICRC standards exist exactly to prevent that. The problem was giving technicians a way to follow them under pressure."

Ford built the first version of RestoreDocAI as an internal tool for his team — a structured checklist generator that embedded IICRC S500 requirements directly into the field workflow. Within three months, claim approval rates at his former employer increased from 71% to over 90%. He left to found RestoreDocAI full-time in early 2023, closing a seed round within six months and a $8.2M Series A in late 2024.

Today RestoreDocAI serves more than 1,200 restoration companies across all 50 states. Ford serves as an advisor to the Restoration Industry Association (RIA) and speaks regularly on AI's role in property insurance claims modernization.

"The insurance system was not built for speed. But it was built for documentation. Every denial I ever saw came down to one thing: the paperwork didn't prove what the field tech actually did."
— Oliver Ford, CEO & Co-Founder, RestoreDocAI
