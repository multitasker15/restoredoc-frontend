Logo Usage

The RestoreDocAI wordmark is our primary brand identifier. The word "Doc" appears in Restoration Blue (#2563eb) against the full company name in Charcoal (#111827). Always use approved lockups — never recreate the logo manually.

Prohibited Logo Usage
RestoreDocAI
Do not alter brand colors
Do not change "Doc" to any color other than #2563eb on approved backgrounds.
RestoreDocAI
Do not italicize

RestoreDocAI
Do not alter weight or spacing
Always use the approved font weight (700) and letter-spacing. No tracking modifications.
Media Usage Rights
  • ✓ Editorial and news coverage may reproduce the RestoreDocAI logo in articles referencing the company.
  • ✓ Podcast cover art and video thumbnails may include the logo with prior written approval.
  • ✓ The company name "RestoreDocAI" should always appear as one word with capital R, D, and AI.
  • ✗ The logo may not be used in advertising, sponsored content, or paid placements without a written license agreement.
  • ✗ Do not place the logo on photographic backgrounds without a solid-color buffer or approved badge variant.
  • ✗ Do not use the logo to imply endorsement of third-party products or services.
